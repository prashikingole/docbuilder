DocBuilder user guide

How to create quotes, agreements and other documents from a Salesforce record, email them, and send them for e-signature.

What DocBuilder does

DocBuilder fills a template, designed by your administrator, with data from the record you are on, and turns it into a PDF. The PDF is saved to the record's Files. You can also email it, or send it for signature with your company's e-signature service.

You only ever see data you already have access to in Salesforce. If a field is hidden from you, it is left out of your document too.

Generate a document

  1. Open the record, for example a Quote or an Opportunity.
  2. Click Generate Document at the top of the page. If you can't see it, it may be under the arrow next to the other buttons; otherwise ask your administrator to add it.
  3. In Templates, tick the template you want. If you tick several, they are combined into one PDF, each starting on a new page.
  4. If the template offers other languages, choose one in Document language.
  5. Under What to do, keep Generate the PDF.
  6. Tick Mark as draft with a watermark if the document is not final. The word DRAFT is printed across each page and added to the file name.
  7. Check the Live preview on the right. It shows the real data from this record.
  8. Click Generate PDF.

When it is done you can Open the file, View the document record, or Generate another.

The line at the bottom of the dialog always says what will happen, for example "Sales Quote · saved to Files on this record". Check it before you click.

Email a document

You need the DocBuilder Email Sender permission. Without it, the email choice does not appear.

  1. In the Generate Document dialog, choose Generate and send by email.
  2. In To, enter one or more addresses separated by commas.
  3. Subject and Message may already be filled in by the template. Change them if you need to.
  4. Click Generate and send.

The PDF is attached to the email and also saved to the record. Your administrator may limit how many people you can email at once, or which email domains are allowed.

Send for signature

Under What to do, each e-signature service your company uses appears as its own choice, for example Send with Dropbox Sign. The template decides who needs to sign; their names and emails are filled in from the record.

Three ways to send

Use the switch at the top of the signing screen to move between them. Your choice is remembered.

ScreenBest for
Compact formQuick sends. Signers, subject and message sit next to the preview.
Guided stepsOccasional sends. Four steps: Document, Signers, Message, Review and send.
Signing deskChecking who signs where. Each person's signing area is outlined in their own colour and number on the document.

Before you send

  • Check every signer has a name and an email. You can correct them here; the record itself is not changed.
  • Choose the Signing order: One after the other sends to signer 1 first, then signer 2 once signer 1 has signed, and so on. Everyone at once sends to all of them together.
  • Check the subject and message. They may already be filled in by the template.

Click Send to N signers. The document goes out a few seconds later.

The signed copy is attached to the record only when everyone has signed. Until then, the copy that was sent is kept on the Signature Request.

Track a signature

Right after sending, and on every Signature Request record, you see:

  • Progress: one line per signer, ticked when they sign. In a one-after-the-other send, only the next person shows as "waiting to sign".
  • Files: the copy that was sent, and the signed copy once it exists.
  • Check status now: asks the e-signature service straight away. Status also updates by itself.
  • Withdraw: cancels the request so nobody else can sign. This cannot be undone.
StatusMeaning
QueuedBeing sent. This takes a few seconds.
Out for signatureWaiting for one or more people.
Signed by everyoneDone. The signed copy is on the record.
DeclinedA signer declined to sign.
WithdrawnCancelled by the sender.
Could not be sentThe e-signature service refused it. The message on the request says why.

Find your documents

  • The PDF: in the Files related list on the record.
  • Everything you generated: the Generated Documents tab in the DocBuilder app. Each entry records when, from which template, and what happened, including any email.
  • Your signature requests: the Signature Requests tab.

You see your own generated documents and signature requests. Administrators can see everyone's.
